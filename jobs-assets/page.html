Definitions
Utility
The tenant each job and asset belongs to. The list holds every utility with at least one job or asset; All utilities includes demo and test tenants.
Open
Jobs whose status is Scheduled, Assigned, EnRoute, InProgress or Paused.
Not scheduled
Jobs with status NotScheduled.
Completed, Validating, Incomplete, Cancelled
The job status of the same name.
Asset status
The asset's own status field: OpenJobs, ClosedJobs or NoJobs.
(none)
The field is blank on those records.
Incomplete reason
Only jobs with a reason recorded, whatever their current stage.
City
Spelling is normalized to title case, so COLUMBUS and Columbus count together.
Drilling down
Clicking a bar keeps only that group on the tab: tiles, both charts and the record list. Other is a mix of smaller groups and cannot be opened.
